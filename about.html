Dolly's of Datchworth · About Us

The team behind Dolly's

Dolly's of Datchworth is owned and run by three friends — Becky, Nikki and Claire — brought together by a shared love of great food, proper coffee and the kind of warmth that makes a café feel like home.

Meet the owners

Three friends, one dream

Nikki

Co-owner

Having worked in hospitality for a number of years, opening a tearoom has always been a dream of mine. I'm so proud to be part of bringing that dream to life with Dolly's — a friendly village spot where everyone can enjoy great coffee, delicious treats and a little time out of their busy day.

Becky

Co-owner

I've always loved the energy of a busy, welcoming café — from the warmth of the kitchen to the buzz of a morning coffee rush. At Dolly's you'll find me in both, whether that's behind the scenes or out front crafting your morning coffee.

Claire

Co-owner & head baker

I love making all the cakes at Dolly's — it's always a joy to be in the kitchen and working with such a great team. I'm always trying new recipes and bakes that match the season, because every visit deserves a little something sweet.

Good food. Great company.
